Hearthbook – Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Publisher: Jakub Kumięga.

In short

Hearthbook has no accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no ads, analytics or trackers.

What stays on your iPhone

Your home profile, appliances, care tasks, costs, photos and documents are stored on your device. Reading appliance labels, receipts and home inspection reports (text recognition) and the Apple Intelligence features, including "Ask your home", run entirely on your device; nothing you scan, import or ask is sent to us or to any AI service.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your data is synced between your devices through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). Only you can access it; we cannot.

Product recall checks

To look for safety recalls, the app sends the type of product (for example "dishwasher") to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's public recall service at saferproducts.gov. Brand, model and serial numbers are matched on your device and are never sent. No information that identifies you is included.

Weather Watch

If you use Weather Watch, the app turns your home's ZIP code into map coordinates with Apple Maps and asks Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for the forecast at those coordinates. Only the ZIP code and the coordinates are sent, without your name or any other information from the app. Apple's privacy policy applies to these requests. The coordinates are kept on your device.

Manual and parts searches

When you tap "Find the manual" or "Find parts and filters", the app opens a Google search in your browser with the appliance's brand, model and type. Nothing is sent until you tap, and Google's privacy policy applies to the search.

Contacts

When you add a service pro from Contacts, you pick one contact in Apple's contact picker and only that contact's name, company, phone number and email are copied into the app. Hearthbook cannot read your address book.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to photograph a label, a receipt, a document or something for your home's cheat sheet (like the water shutoff). Images are kept in the app and in your iCloud, never uploaded to us.

Notifications

Reminders, including weather alerts, are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Children

Hearthbook is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/kumi20/hearthbook-legal/issues.
